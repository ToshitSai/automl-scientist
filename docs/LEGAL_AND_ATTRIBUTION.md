### 1. License Preservation (Section 3.1)

### 2. Legal Use Restrictions (Section 3.2)
Derivative work must NOT be used for:
- **Surveillance**: Detecting or inferring legally protected classes or personal features/identities.
- **Computer Generated Media**: Synthesizing audio/video realistic representations without watermarks/captions.
- **Health Care**: Predicting insurance claim likelihood or diagnosing medical conditions without human oversight.
- **Criminal**: Predicting criminal likelihood based on personal or facial data.
- **Scientific Manuscripts & Academic Integrity (Section 3.2.e)**: Generating scientific manuscripts, papers, or technical reports without expressly and intelligibly disclaiming machine generation.
